# How to explain this demo to colleagues

> Pitch Practice is a sales-coaching demo built on Interhuman's audio-only
> analysis API. You pick a buyer — a freight VP, a hospital clinical director,
> a fintech head of engineering, a grocery ops director, or a CFO — get a
> 20-second briefing on what you're selling, and pitch them over voice; the AI
> buyer talks back in character, with its own budget, stack, and objections.
> While you speak, Interhuman's realtime endpoint reads your *delivery* from
> audio alone — hesitation, stress, confidence, monologuing — and the buyer
> genuinely reacts to it: stumble on the price and they push back, ramble and
> they go flat. The screen shows what the audio hears in real time (live
> signals, a call-trajectory graph, terse nudges), and when you hang up, a
> coach grades the call — score, the pivotal moments with timestamps, what
> worked, what to fix, and one drill for your next call. The point: everything
> the buyer and the coach "noticed" came from a single audio stream and one
> API — no camera, no wearables.
